# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads align technical direction, delivery execution, and architectural quality across the project team. They help turn roadmap priorities into workable engineering plans and ensure the team can deliver safely and predictably.

### Responsibilities
- Translate product and project priorities into engineering plans
- Guide architecture, technical trade-offs, and implementation standards
- Coordinate cross-team technical dependencies and sequencing
- Support engineering capacity planning and milestone readiness
- Partner with developers to reduce technical risk and unblock delivery

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable, and reliable solutions
- Improve predictability of technical execution
- Balance business needs with engineering quality and sustainability

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning meetings
- Dependency and risk discussions with PMs and stakeholders
- Team-level standups and implementation decisions

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work closely with Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Partner with Project Managers on scope, milestones, and delivery risk
- Guide Developers on implementation quality, standards, and technical decision-making

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners help ensure that projects meet required policies, privacy expectations, and risk standards throughout delivery. They act as a review and advisory function for secure, compliant execution.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements early in the project
- Review architectures, workflows, and data handling for risk
- Support mitigation plans for vulnerabilities, access controls, and policy gaps
- Coordinate review and approval steps with stakeholders and engineering leads
- Track unresolved compliance risks and escalation paths

### Goals
- Reduce product and operational risk
- Improve trust, governance, and policy adherence
- Support safe delivery without slowing critical work unnecessarily

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, compliance check-ins, and risk assessments
- Escalations for high-risk findings or incident follow-up
- Collaboration with PMs and engineering leads during planning and release readiness

### How they interact with existing roles
- Partner with Product Managers on risk-informed prioritization and customer trust concerns
- Work with Project Managers to incorporate compliance checkpoints into delivery plans
- Support Developers by reviewing secure implementation patterns and required controls

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Advocate

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Stakeholder Advocates represent customer needs, business context, and stakeholder concerns in project planning and execution. They help ensure the team remains grounded in real user impact and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and synthesize customer feedback, pain points, and expectations
- Represent stakeholder priorities during prioritization and trade-off discussions
- Help identify business impact and success criteria for releases
- Support change communication and adoption readiness
- Surface customer-facing risks or blockers early to the project team

### Goals
- Improve customer value and satisfaction
- Increase alignment between business goals and delivery outcomes
- Reduce surprises during launch or rollout

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings, customer feedback sessions, and release readiness reviews
- Weekly project updates and escalation conversations
- Collaboration with PMs and Product Managers to refine prioritization

### How they interact with existing roles
- Provide customer context and urgency signals to Product Managers
- Help Project Managers understand stakeholder expectations and communication needs
- Inform Developers and Engineering Leads about real-world usage needs and edge cases

---

## Release Manager / Operations Owner

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Operations Owners coordinate deployment readiness, change communication, and operational continuity. They help ensure releases are executed smoothly and that service health is monitored before, during, and after go-live.

### Responsibilities
- Define release readiness criteria and deployment windows
- Coordinate deployment sequencing, rollback planning, and monitoring
- Align engineering, support, and stakeholders around change communication
- Capture operational issues and post-release follow-up actions
- Support incident response and recovery when issues arise after deployment

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and service disruption
- Improve operational readiness and observability
- Ensure smoother stakeholder communication during launches

### Typical Communication
- Release planning sessions, runbooks, and post-deployment check-ins
- Incident updates, rollback decisions, and stakeholder notifications
- Collaboration with PMs, Engineering Leads, and support teams

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Project Managers to track release timing and dependencies
- Coordinate with Engineering Leads on technical readiness and rollback criteria
- Partner with Product Managers to ensure launches align with stakeholder expectations
- Support Developers and QA by confirming release validation and operational checks
